## 👋 Hey, I'm Mohit

I'm a **Software Engineer & Computer Science graduate** who enjoys working at the intersection of **backend engineering and intelligent products**.

I started with **Java and Spring Boot**, building REST APIs and database-driven applications. Since then, my work has expanded into **Generative AI**, where I've worked on everything from evaluating AI models and designing generation pipelines to building the backend systems that make those experiences work reliably in production.

I like engineering problems where there is something meaningful to **design, optimize, automate, or build from scratch**.
